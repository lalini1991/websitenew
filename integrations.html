Integrations

CMDR connects to the systems your hospital already runs - on both sides of an incident: where it is detected, and where your team responds.

Where incidents are detected

CMDR watches the sources, so your team does not have to

Signals from monitoring, the call centre, security and ticketing are correlated into one incident instead of four separate investigations.

SW

SolarWinds

Network monitoring. Latency, saturation and device alerts start the incident workflow.

TD

TalkDesk

Call centre. A spike in service desk call volume is itself a detection signal.

SEC

Security monitoring

Alerts from your security stack are correlated with the operational picture.

JI

Jira

Ticket creation, categorisation, prioritisation and assignment - plus RCA tickets.

Where your team responds

The bridge opens where your team already works

No new console to learn during an outage. CMDR runs the response inside the collaboration tools your hospital has already approved.

MT

Microsoft Teams

Incident channels and bridges created and staffed automatically from the on-call rota.

ZM

Zoom

Bridge calls opened, recorded and transcribed for the root cause report.

OL

Outlook

Automated IT alert email and leadership status updates, on the cadence you set.

JI

Jira

The incident record and RCA ticket stay in the system your team already files in.

SL

Slack

Start, update and close an incident, and generate incident reports, with one command.

ⓘ Connections to SolarWinds, TalkDesk, the security monitoring stack and Jira are built in Phase 1 of a CMDR engagement. Talk to us about the timeline for your environment.

Installing CMDR

CMDR is installed into your Teams or Slack tenant during onboarding, with the permission scopes reviewed and agreed with your security team first. We will walk your reviewers through exactly what each scope is used for.
