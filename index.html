The problem

In most hospitals, major incident data lives across Jira, SolarWinds, TalkDesk and the security stack. There is no single source of truth, no KPI for how long a response takes, and no measure of what an outage costs.

What CMDR does

CMDR consolidates every source into one deterministic system, standardises the response, and learns the precursors to the next outage. Getting ahead of even one significant outage a year more than pays for the platform.
