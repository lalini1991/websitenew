Features

Three things CMDR does, inside the systems your hospital already runs.

Workflow automation

Declare, staff and brief an incident in one step

CMDR triggers your major incident protocols in Jira, creates and categorises the ticket, engages the IT duty officer and the right team on-calls in Microsoft Teams, and sends the automated IT alert email through Outlook.

Incidents are linked to one another, so a recurring failure shows up as a pattern rather than as five unrelated tickets.

Learning & RCA

The root cause report drafts itself

CMDR ingests the Teams call recording in real time, opens and assigns the RCA ticket in Jira, and drafts the report from what was actually said and done. It goes to IT leadership, and into the training material for your team.

Your analysts review and sign off instead of reconstructing the morning from memory a week later.

Resolution insights

Know where the response loses time

KPIs where there were none

Time to detect, time to engage and time to resolve, measured consistently rather than estimated afterwards.

No single point of knowledge

Every incident and resolution is recorded and searchable, so the response never depends on one person.

The true cost of an incident

Call volume, affected clinical services and response hours in one view, so leadership sees what an outage cost.
