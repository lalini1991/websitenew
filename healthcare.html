Hospitals run on systems that were never designed to fail together. CMDR makes the response to that failure consistent, fast and recorded - every time.

The problem

No single source of truth, and no KPI for the response

Today

In most hospitals, major incident data lives across Jira, SolarWinds, TalkDesk and the security stack. There is no single source of truth, no KPI for how long a response takes, and no measure of what an outage costs. When the EHR degrades, the first ten minutes go on working out who to call.

With CMDR

CMDR consolidates every source into one deterministic system, standardises the response, and learns the precursors to the next outage. Getting ahead of even one significant outage a year more than pays for the platform.

Built for healthcare

What "built for hospitals" actually means

A HIPAA-compliant boundary

CMDR leverages AI and machine learning in a fully HIPAA-compliant environment. Atlassian Jira's AI solutions are not currently HIPAA compliant.

Your systems, not ours

Connectors to Jira, SolarWinds, TalkDesk, Microsoft Teams, Outlook and your security monitoring stack. Nothing is ripped out and replaced.

A service layer

We define with you what to measure and how incidents are classified, then configure it - rather than handing over an empty platform.

The engagement

Visibility, then standardisation, then avoidance

Year 1

Visibility

Every ticketing, call and monitoring source connected. KPIs for major incident response where none existed. Leadership sees the state of the environment and what each incident costs.

Year 2

Standardisation

Incident bridges, recordings and tickets created automatically. Training built from your own incidents, so new IT staff are productive sooner and the response no longer depends on who is on shift.

Year 3

Avoidance Phase 5 · on the roadmap

With enough incident history, CMDR identifies the precursor conditions to a major incident and flags them before the outage. Avoiding one significant outage a year more than justifies the investment; every year after that, the saving repeats. Scheduled for Q4 FY27.

Why this is not already solved

The incumbents sell a platform, not a process

ServiceNow and Jira Service Management are horizontal: they are sold to every industry, so the incident process, the classification scheme and the compliance boundary are left to the customer to define - which is exactly the work a hospital IT department does not have the capacity to do. Because hospitals run substantially the same stack as one another, the process CMDR standardises at one hospital transfers to the next without being rebuilt.
