The engagement

Quarterly milestones, with measurable goals for each phase. Low-risk, high-reward objectives come first.

Phase 1 · Q4 FY’26

Data connections

Identify and connect every ticketing, call and monitoring source to CMDR.

Assess data in a deterministic system many times faster than people can.

Phase 2 · Q1 FY’27

Data analysis

Establish KPIs where none exist today.

The return

Where the saving comes from

Each phase returns something on its own, and produces the data that makes the next one possible.

Immediately

Automated initiation, bridge management and RCA remove the human error and inconsistency that affect response times today - and the hours spent assembling a timeline after the fact.

Within a year

Standardised, recorded processes cut process overhead and shorten onboarding, so a response stops depending on who is on shift or who remembers last time.

Long term, and compounding

Every incident CMDR records adds to the pattern history. With enough of it, the machine learning layer flags the precursors to a major incident while there is still time to act - and an outage that is avoided costs nothing. The history only gets richer, so each year of data makes the next year's avoidance better than the last.

Getting ahead of even one significant outage a year more than justifies the investment.

Why this is not already solved

The incumbents sell a platform, not a process

ServiceNow and Jira Service Management are horizontal: they leave the incident process, the classification scheme and the compliance boundary to the customer - which is exactly the work a hospital IT department has no capacity for. And because hospitals run substantially the same stack, what CMDR standardises at one transfers to the next.
